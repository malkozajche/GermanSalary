Practical explanations (2026) • compact • mobile-friendly

Multiple employers / Tax class VI ▾

Tax class VI typically applies when you have multiple incomes and at least one additional employment is above the current mini-job income limit (e.g. > 603 €).

You can find your tax class on your payslip. Your employer retrieves the electronic wage tax deduction features (ELStAM) from the tax office via your tax ID.

Health insurance (KV) & Zusatzbeitrag ▾

The stated KV rates may include both employee and employer shares. The Zusatzbeitrag depends on your health insurer. Since 2019, employers and employees share the Zusatzbeitrag equally.

This calculator uses the employee's half of the entered Zusatzbeitrag for the KV calculation.

Insurance threshold (PKV) 2026 ▾

Above the compulsory insurance threshold, an employee may under certain conditions opt out of statutory health insurance and switch to private health insurance (PKV). Often the income must exceed the threshold for three consecutive years (details vary by situation).

For 2026, the compulsory insurance threshold is 77,400 € per year.

Care insurance (PV) – employee share ▾

Since January 1, 2025, the care insurance contribution rate is 3.6% (previously 3.4% since July 2023). The childless surcharge is 0.6%. The rate also depends on the number of children under 25.
